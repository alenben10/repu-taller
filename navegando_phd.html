Cosas que NO hacer

Hacer de tu Personal Statement un CV en prosa: listar logros sin contar una historia.
Usar clichés comunes y vagos como “Since I was a child I loved science” en tu Research Statement.
Usar “we” en lugar de “I” cuando describes tus experiencias de investigación.
Escribir un fit genérico, sin nombrar profesores ni laboratorios del programa.
Enviar el mismo texto a todos los programas y dejar por error el nombre de otra universidad.
Dejar que la IA escriba por ti: pierdes tu voz y algunos programas lo prohíben (ej. UCSD).

Rayos X de un párrafo real del Research Statement

OBJETIVODuring the second half of my sophomore year (2022), I worked under his guidance on developing a low-cost electrode based on Nafion-stabilized silver nanoparticles supported on fluorine-doped tin oxide for the electrochemical detection of copper and lead ions in water bodies. CONTEXTOConventional methods for detecting heavy metals are prohibitively expensive, highlighting the need for portable, cost-effective alternatives. MÉTODOSI conducted electrochemical and structural characterization using cyclic voltammetry and scanning electron microscopy, optimizing analytical parameters using differential pulse anodic stripping voltammetry (DPV). DESAFÍOS Y SOLUCIÓNWe faced challenges with the electrode's electrochemical stability during DPV, which we mitigated by modifying the surface with Nafion. Ensuring the optimal voltage range to prevent oxidation interference was another hurdle we overcame. RESULTADOOur electrode achieved detection limits of 8.87 ppb for Pb(II) and 3.26 ppb for Cu(II), comparable to standard ICP-MS methods. IMPACTOThese results, published in PLOS One in 2025, offer a viable solution for environmental monitoring in remote areas like the Andean regions. DIFUSIÓNWe presented our work as a poster presentation at the 30th Peruvian Congress of Chemistry SILAE and the XXIV Scientific Conferences at UPCH, where we won 2nd place. QUÉ SE LLEVAAlthough research in environmental electrochemistry is not what I intend to pursue, this experience reaffirmed my motivation to continue in research and helped me to clarify my interests.

Cómo conectar tus ideas y hacer transiciones efectivas entre párrafos

Y

Una experiencia complementa o amplía a otra
“Building on this…” · “In parallel…” · “This complemented…”
EJEMPLO DE ALEN“My coursework in calculus, linear algebra, probability, and statistics has complemented this experience, giving me the quantitative foundation to understand and apply machine learning methods.”

PERO

Aparece un problema o un giro, y cuentas cómo respondiste
“However…” · “Although…” · “…, but…”
EJEMPLO DE BELÉN“Working with eukaryotic genomes posed challenges due to intronic regions, but by refining our bioinformatics pipeline, we established reliable open reading frames.”

POR LO TANTO

Una experiencia te llevó a la siguiente
“That same question became…” · “This led me to…”
EJEMPLO DE ALEN“In 2024, that same question became my undergraduate thesis.”
Evita conectores solo cronológicos: “Then, in 2023…” · “After that…”

Un “however” muestra cómo resolviste un problema

El plan

PERO…
“However, with more than 20,000 papers to analyze, API costs and processing speed posed substantial limitations.”
Mi decisión
“I began learning to run LLMs locally on the NSF-supported Bridges-2 system and developed FuncMap, a fully local, multistep workflow…”
Resultado
“…that processed 5,000 papers in less than a day… with no API costs… more than 95% precision in manual validation.”

Qué es y qué no es un Personal Statement

Es

  • La historia de por qué y cómo llegaste aquí
  • Tu motivación por la ciencia
  • Qué obstáculos superaste
  • Tus aspiraciones profesionales a futuro: en quién te quieres convertir

No es

  • Una biografía completa
  • Un listado de las experiencias de tu CV
  • Un diario de emociones escrito con recursos líricos
Regla: cada momento personal debe conectar con tu ciencia o tu comunidad.

Un buen gancho es una escena auténtica

“⓪ It is said that, usually, a child can develop two radically opposite kinds of aspirations: follow the same or a completely different path as their parents. For most of my life I was the second kind of child. Both of my parents are scientists in Peru, my father a physicist and my mother a biologist… dinners together were full of discoveries like: ① -Mom, why should I wash my hands after petting Whisky? -"Well Alencito, there exists these little very little things called bacteria..." ② -Dad, how can I score a free kick goal? -"Let's calculate the best kicking angle!". ③ As fun as it sounds, being a scientist in Peru also comes with very high financial hurdles and instability…”
0Idea universal + giro personal: “the second kind” crea suspenso
1Escena concreta con diálogo
2Humor y humanidad (Whisky era nuestro perro)
3Introduce una TENSIÓN: amor por la ciencia vs miedo a la inestabilidad
→El ensayo entero resolverá esa tensión

Ejemplo: un obstáculo grande contado con acciones

“Back in Peru my father—the sole economic support of our family—lost all his jobs… Simultaneously, my mother was diagnosed with multiple malignant skin carcinomas. I was compelled to interrupt my studies in Vancouver and return to my homeland. … Undeterred, at the age of 19, I secured a position as a research intern in the R&D department at Corporacion Montana… I conducted cell viability assays using bioactive compounds from medicinal plants. … Determined to continue my education, I earned a merit-based Excellency Scholarship to join the inaugural class studying Environmental Engineering at… Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia.”
1Contexto en pocas oraciones, sin dramatizar
2Acción concreta: a los 19, una pasantía de I+D
3Resultado: beca de excelencia, nueva carrera
→El obstáculo explica el camino; no pide lástima
